## 🎫 The Customer Support Portal grows up: requests, conversations, and submissions

When the portal's passwordless sign-in landed, customers could get in — and that was about it. This release delivers the experience inside: everything a customer needs to raise, follow, and finish a support request without ever writing an email.

### 📋 My Requests

Signing in now lands your customer on **My Requests** — every request they've raised with you, with a **search box** and **status filter** for finding the one they mean. Each row shows where the request stands in plain, customer-facing terms.

### 💬 Follow the conversation — and reply

Opening a request shows its **conversation**: your agents' public replies and the customer's own messages in one thread. A status chip (for example **Awaiting your reply**) makes it obvious at a glance whose move is next.

From there a customer can **reply** directly — the message lands on the ticket for your team just as an email reply would — and **attach files**, each one **automatically scanned for malware** before your team ever sees it.

### ✏️ Update details and mark it solved

If you expose fields to customers on a request's form, the customer can fill in or correct those details right from the portal — internal fields never appear. And when their problem is sorted, they can **mark the request solved** themselves.

### 📝 Submit a request from inside the portal

The new **Submit request** page lists the request forms you choose to **publish to the portal**, so a signed-in customer can raise a new request on the spot. Listing is per-form and off by default — you decide exactly which forms appear.
