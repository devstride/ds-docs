## 📚 Knowledge Base — five fixes from your reports

- **New Article files where you are.** Select a category in the sidebar and click **New Article**: the draft is filed in that category instead of landing in *Uncategorized*.
- **Archived articles leave the "All" list.** The manage list's **All** now shows drafts and published articles only; archived ones stay one click away under the **Archived** filter. Reordering a category still works when it holds archived articles, and a category with nothing active says **No active articles to show** rather than inviting a first article.
- **Create-only roles can write their drafts.** A member whose role only has *Create knowledge-base articles* used to open a new article they could not type into. They can now write, file, and keep editing their own drafts; editing after publishing needs *Edit your own* or *Publish*. See [Knowledge Base permissions](/major-modules/knowledge-base/knowledge-base-permissions) — the permission descriptions in role settings say the same.
- **The Audience panel is always there.** Visibility and Companies are shown to every editor; without the *Publish* permission they are read-only with a hint saying who can change them, so a missing permission no longer looks like a missing feature.
- **Board cards open on a click.** On kanban boards a tiny mouse wobble during a click used to start a drag and swallow the click — noticed most while screen-sharing. A press now needs a few pixels of deliberate movement before it becomes a drag.

## 💬 Comments

- A comment deep link opens the comment it points to even when the item's stored comment count is out of date.
- Opening a different item after arriving from a comment notification no longer carries that comment link along — the new item's discussion behaves normally (and its "write a comment" box appears when the thread is empty). A comment box you opened yourself is never closed by a later notification.
