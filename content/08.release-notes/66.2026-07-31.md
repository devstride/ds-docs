## 🧩 Templates

Some work has the same shape every time. Onboarding a client, launching a product, running a compliance review — the names and dates change, the structure doesn't. **Templates** let you save that structure once and create it again in a few clicks.

A template is a tree of workstreams and work items. Each node carries the fields a real item carries — description, dates, team, assignee, board or cycle, priority, tags, estimate, and the custom fields configured for that work type. Apply the template under any valid parent and DevStride creates the whole tree, correctly nested, in one go.

**Templates** is a new item in the sidebar under **Admin**.

### Variables — one template, many situations

The parts that change per use become **variables**: questions the template asks when it's applied. Eight types are available — Date, User, Team, Board, Text, Number, Options and Custom field — and a template can ask up to 20 questions.

- **Text, Number, Date and Options** can be dropped into titles and descriptions as `{{tokens}}`, so a single template produces *"Northwind Bank onboarding"* this week and *"Acme Savings onboarding"* next week.
- **Date variables carry day offsets**, so you ask for one anchor date — the launch, the review, the go-live — and every other date in the template places itself relative to it. Change the anchor and the whole plan moves.
- **User variables can be scoped to a team**, including a team chosen in an earlier question, so the person picker only ever offers people who could actually do the work.
- **Board placement** can be fixed, asked for, or resolved against a **cadence** — "whichever cycle is current when this is applied" — so a template doesn't go stale when a cycle closes.

### Try it before anything exists

The builder has a **Try it** playground that simulates an apply live as you type: fill in the values and watch the titles resolve and the dates shift, with nothing created and nothing saved. A **Timeline** view draws the template as a relative schedule — days from the anchor date — so you can check the shape before real dates exist.

The apply wizard itself previews for real. Step 3 shows exactly the tree that will be created, resolves every reference against your organization, and reports anything that would block it — a missing required value, an assignee outside the scoped team, a cadence with no open cycle — *before* anything is written.

### The fast way to author one: capture existing work

You almost certainly already have work with the right shape. **Save as Template…**, on the right-click menu of any workstream or work item, turns that subtree — every child, at every depth, including sub-items — into a template with all its values captured as fixed. Open the builder, promote the handful that should vary into variables, and you're done in minutes rather than rebuilding the tree by hand.

### Sharing

Every template starts **private to you**. Share it with named people, with whole teams, or with the entire organization, at **Can use** (see and apply) or **Can edit**. An explicit **No access** entry for one person overrides a grant they'd get from a team or the organization — so "the whole Engineering team except one person" is a single setting, not a rebuild.

Sharing stays with the owner: an editor can change a template's structure but not who it's shared with.

### Templates stay honest about your configuration

Templates reference your real work types, custom fields, priorities, tags, teams, boards and people rather than copying them. So DevStride keeps both ends in sync:

- Before you delete or archive a piece of configuration, the confirmation tells you how many templates use it — and uses the right verb. An **irreversible delete** (custom field, option, tag, priority) **removes** the reference; a **reversible archive** (work type, team, board, folder, member) only **flags** the templates for attention. The count is organization-wide and honest, while other members' private templates are counted without being named.
- Templates carry a **health** badge. A template whose references no longer resolve can't be applied until it's fixed, and the builder names the exact node and field that needs attention.

### Permissions

Templates ship with their own permissions in **Settings → Permissions**: **Access Templates** (on the default Member role), **Create Item Template**, **Manage Own Item Templates** and **Manage All Item Templates**. Applying a template also requires the ordinary permission to create items where you're applying it — it's not a way around item permissions.

### Available to your AI assistant

The whole feature is exposed through the [DevStride MCP Server](/devstride-mcp-server/what-is-the-devstride-mcp-server), so you can ask your assistant to find, capture, adjust, preview and apply templates — within the permissions and shares you already have.
