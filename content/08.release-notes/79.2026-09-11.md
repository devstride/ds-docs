## ⏱️ Time tracking: flag late entries, choose who sees time

- **Soft Close marks late time instead of blocking it.** Time logged after a period's grace window
  still saves, flagged **Late**, so managers see it without anyone being locked out. It runs
  alongside Time Tracking Lock — Lock monthly and Soft Close weekly, say.
  📚 [Time Tracking Lock and Soft Close](/workspace-administration/settings/organization/time-tracking-lock)
- **Time Entry Access, role by role.** Switch it off and that role no longer sees or logs time: no
  Time Tracking tab, Time Spent section, time column or card badge. Reports and exports are unaffected.
  📚 [Roles and permissions](/workspace-administration/permissions/roles-and-permissions)

## ⚙️ Automations that tidy up after you

- **Subtasks follow their parents.** With **Automate New/Done** on, completing an item completes its
  subtasks, and reopening a subtask reopens its parent. A parent completes on its own only once
  everything under it is done.
- **Clear out work that has sat in Done.** A new automation trigger acts on items that have been in
  Done on a board for a set number of hours or days — move them on, or take them off the board.
- **Archived boards can be deleted for good**, once you type the board's name. Their items stay in
  their workstreams.

## 📈 Charts you can trust

- **Burndown projections are realistic again.** Work added mid-range no longer pushes the projected
  finish months out, and the current period shows progress so far plus a projected end.
  📚 [Burn down](/major-modules/measure-performance-reports/burn-down)
- **The Tag filter now filters** every analytics report, time tracking included. Saved views with a
  Tag filter will show different numbers, because they were never actually filtered before.
- **Flow charts no longer squash** into the left edge early in the month.

## ✨ Also new

- **Reorder rows from the keyboard.** Tab to the grab handle, Space to pick up, arrows to move, Space
  to drop — announced to screen readers — on board tables, child items, subtasks, the Knowledge Base
  and settings lists.
- **Tables follow dark themes** instead of showing as a bright white block.
- **CSV exports are safe to open.** A value a spreadsheet would run as a formula is saved as text, so
  a hostile item title can't run when the file opens. Excel shows the leading apostrophe; Google
  Sheets hides it.

## 🎫 Service Desk

- **Forwarded replies land on the right ticket.** When a colleague forwards a customer's reply to
  something the desk sent, it joins that ticket instead of opening a duplicate.
  📚 [Email channels](/major-modules/service-desk/email-channels)
- **Requests staff file for a customer are live immediately** — no surprise verification email.
- **A new "Converted to ticket" notification**, off by default, in everyone's notification settings.
- **The email confirmation link no longer hangs** on a spinning logo.

## 🧹 Quality improvements

Loads of small quality improvements — the kind you'd only notice if they were still broken:

- Public request forms no longer go blank as someone types.
- Wide comments scroll instead of being cut off.
- Long Knowledge Base articles scroll inside the page again.
- An article's history always marks the right version as Current.
- The Activity tab loads for items with a very long history.
- Items linked to many GitHub commits no longer slow down edits or lists.
- Large boards now recognise when every item has loaded.
- Pull request checks show one current result per check, as GitHub does.
- Images with very long file names no longer stop attachments updating.
- Relationships and board links work on Jira and Azure DevOps imported items.
- Jira and Azure DevOps syncs now move dependent items' dates.
- New Jira connections verify every update; re-select your project to protect an existing one.
- People invited who sign in with Google or SSO keep their real name.
- Admins can hide installed plugins from a role's menu.
- In new organizations, the Admin role can switch Service Desk on.
- Portal items opened from a shared list show their pictures.
- Replies from staff who raised their own ticket count as the customer's.
- Bounced and spam-flagged ticket emails are marked, and repeat sends stopped.
- Converting an item to a ticket can no longer half-finish.
- Erasing a customer also removes what their emails and form uploads created.
- A picture removed from a form before submitting isn't kept.
- Slack messages containing a blank image now arrive.
- Slack shows quotation marks as typed, not as codes.
- Map links say "workstream"; old links and saved views still work.
- **API and MCP:** an organization's keys share a limit of 1,000 requests a minute, with HTTP 429 and
  Retry-After beyond it.
- **API and MCP:** "folder" names are now "workstream"; the old names keep working until at least
  1 October 2026.
- **API and MCP:** searches say when results were cut short, and large list pages may return fewer
  rows than requested.
- **API and MCP:** archiving many items or workstreams in one call works; the permission summary is
  capped at 5,000 items.
  📚 [API keys](/getting-started/admin-onboarding-getting-started/api-keys/api-keys)
