## ⏱️ Choose where your team records time

Want time recorded against the work being done, rather than its parent Epic or Feature? Administrators can now turn off **Allow Parent Item Time Entries** under **Settings → Organization → Time Tracking → General**.

The switch is **on by default**, so your current workflow continues unchanged. Turn it off to allow new entries only on work types with no child types. This follows your work-type structure, even when a particular parent item has no children yet. Existing parent-item time entries remain available to view, edit, or delete.

📚 [Configure time entry rules](/workspace-administration/settings/organization/time-tracking-lock#allow-parent-item-time-entries)

## 👀 See more without opening every item

The **Description** column brings useful context into Items, My Work, board tables, and Child Items. Scan a plain-text preview, then click the cell to read or edit the full description. Your saved column choices stay intact; enable **Description** in **Table columns** if it is not already visible.

Cycle filters also stay focused on your current board view. Choosing a cycle no longer pulls in unrelated boards from other teams.

📚 [Work with table descriptions](/major-modules/workstreams-and-work-items/manage-items-table#reading-and-editing-descriptions)

## ✍️ Keep your words while you work

If a description cannot be saved, your draft stays in the open drawer so you can correct it and try again. List refreshes no longer falsely report that someone else edited the description or replace your draft.

Long discussion comments now scroll inside the editor while the formatting toolbar and **Cancel** and **Comment** buttons stay within reach. Very large descriptions and comments receive a clear size-limit message when saved; shorten the text or attach the longer material as a file.

📚 [Explore item editing](/getting-started/admin-onboarding-getting-started/item-workspace-tour/tabs)

## 🔑 Fewer sign-in interruptions

Temporary connection or sign-in service problems no longer unnecessarily sign you out. When a company single sign-on session really has expired or been rejected, DevStride returns you to sign-in instead of leaving the app stuck.

Administrators configuring single sign-on can also save a newly typed email domain without pressing Enter first. Domain verification is still required.

## 💬 Smoother customer requests

Request forms now keep explicit **No** and **0** answers, even when a different default was configured. Required calculated fields no longer prevent submission; DevStride fills them from their formulas after the item is created.

Customers who try the wrong company's support portal can receive an email pointing them to their own portal, where they sign in normally.

📚 [Set up request forms](/major-modules/service-desk/request-forms)

## 👥 Easier team setup and article review

Use **Add multiple users** when creating or editing a team to choose several people at once. Existing members stay in place.

Knowledge Base version previews now open full-screen, making longer articles easier to review before deciding whether to restore them.
