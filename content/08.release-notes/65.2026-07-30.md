## 🔑 Sign in with Google

The login page now offers **Sign in with Google**. There's nothing for members to set up — DevStride matches the Google account to the member by **verified email address**:

- An existing member's first Google sign-in **links** the two accounts; password sign-in keeps working exactly as before.
- An **invited** user can accept their invitation by signing in with Google — no password setup required.
- A Google account that doesn't match any member is **refused with a clear message** — no account is ever created from an unmatched sign-in, and every rejection tells the visitor what to do next.
- **Signing out signs you all the way out**: after sign-out, the next "Sign in with Google" asks which account to use rather than silently reusing the last one — important on shared machines.

::alert{type="note" title="MFA-required organizations"}
Organizations that **require multi-factor authentication** keep their guarantee: Google sign-ins don't pass through DevStride's MFA challenge, so members of those organizations continue to sign in with password + MFA.
::

📚 Learn more: [**Users, Roles & MFA**](/workspace-administration/settings/organization/users-roles-and-mfa)

## 🎫 Portal polish: inline images, grouped filters, refreshed sign-in

- **Inline email images now render** where they belong — in the portal's request conversation and in the agent drawer — instead of appearing as broken references. Customers and agents both see the message as it was sent.
- The portal's **request filters are grouped** and the page column is bounded, so My Requests stays tidy on wide screens.
- The **portal sign-in page has been redesigned** — a cleaner auth card, clearer type hierarchy, and balanced actions.
