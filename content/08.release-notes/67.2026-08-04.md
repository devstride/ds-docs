## 🏢 Enterprise SSO — sign in through your company's identity provider

Organizations can now connect **their own identity provider** — Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace, or any OIDC/SAML IdP — and have their team sign in through the company's own corporate login:

- **Bring your IdP.** Owners register a provider under **Settings → Organization → Identity Providers**, with OIDC (recommended) or SAML. 📚 [Enterprise SSO](/workspace-administration/settings/organization/enterprise-sso)
- **Prove your domain, own your sign-ins.** Email domains are claimed with a **DNS verification record**, so routing is anchored to proof of domain ownership — a verified domain is yours exclusively, across all of DevStride.
- **Discovery on the login page.** Anyone typing an email on a verified domain sees a **"Continue with [your company] SSO"** button in place of the password field; everyone else's login page is unchanged.
- **Automatic member provisioning.** Optionally, people on your verified domain who were never invited can sign in and get an account created on the spot, with a default role you choose.

## 🪟 Sign in with Microsoft

Anyone with a **personal Microsoft account** (outlook.com, hotmail.com, live.com…) can now sign in with it, exactly like the Google button: it links to your existing DevStride account by email, accepts pending invitations, and never creates an account for an unmatched address. One person can link both Google and Microsoft. Company work accounts are served by Enterprise SSO instead. 📚 [Signing in with Google or Microsoft](/workspace-administration/settings/organization/users-roles-and-mfa)

## 🔧 Also in this release

- **Clearer sign-in diagnostics** — when a stored integration credential is rejected, the API now says why, instead of a generic failure.
- **Sturdier deploys and integrations** — deploy-status reporting is accurate again, Jira imports report real account identities and isolate per-organization failures, and internal auth housekeeping got stricter teardown guarantees.
