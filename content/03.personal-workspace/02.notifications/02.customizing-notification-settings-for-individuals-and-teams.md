---
title: "Customizing Notification Settings for Individuals and Teams"
description: "Choose direct-mention channels and conditional notifications for your role in each item."
---

Choose where DevStride sends direct mentions and which item activities should notify you. Each person manages their own preferences for the selected organization. Direct mentions and conditional activity notifications have separate controls.

For example, as a project manager, you may want to receive real-time notifications whenever a critical task is completed or requires urgent attention. Customizing your notifications allows you to receive instant alerts when a task moves from "In Progress" to "Review" or "Done," enabling you to promptly review completed work, provide feedback, and keep the project on track.

#### Receiving Notifications 

Open your account menu, then **My Account → Notification Settings** to edit your preferences for the selected organization.

Under **Notify Me of Direct Mentions Via**, select the channels you want:

- DevStride Notification Center
- Slack
- Teams (available after your organization connects Microsoft Teams; personal app installation is also required)
- Email

#### Conditional Push Notifications 

Under **Send Conditional Push Notifications To**, select a channel tab:

- DevStride Notification Center
- Email
- Slack
- Microsoft Teams (its own tab, shown once your organization has connected a Teams tenant)

Each channel has its own grid. Check the intersection of an activity row and your role on an item. For example, **Assignee Modified → Assignee** selects assignment changes for items assigned to you. Preferences save as you change them.

By way of role of team member, select one or more:

- Assignee
- Creator
- Watcher
- Participant

Then, also set your notification preferences based on when certain activities occur, such as:

- Timers Modified
- Participants Modified
- Custom Fields Modified
- Time Estimate Modified
- Status Modified
- Dependencies Modified
- Item Created or Deleted
- Parent Workstream Modified
- Pull Request Activity
- Assignee Modified
- Description Modified
- Assets Modified
- Comments Modified
- Point Effort Modified
- Cycle Modified
- Team Modified
- Tags Modified
- Board Modified

#### Enable and test Microsoft Teams

Your administrator first [connects the Microsoft tenant and uploads the DevStride app](/workspace-administration/settings/integrations/microsoft-teams#connect-the-tenant). Once the app is available:

1. Sign in to Teams with your work or school account in the connected organization.
2. Open **Apps → Built for your org → DevStride → Add**. Review the permissions and terms, then open its personal chat. The **DevStride** app is for production; **DevStride Dev** is for shared development.
3. Confirm that your Microsoft email matches your verified DevStride email and that your DevStride organization membership is active.
4. In **My Account → Notification Settings**, check **Teams** under **Notify Me of Direct Mentions Via**. This is off by default.
5. Ask another member of the same DevStride organization to type **@**, select your name, and post a new comment on an item you can access. Self-mentions do not notify you.
6. Look for the message in your personal **DevStride** Teams chat. Its link should open the correct production item at `app.devstride.com`.

The disabled bot composer and **You cannot send messages to this bot** message are expected: this app delivers notifications and does not require a greeting or command.

For other updates, separately select the **Teams** tab under **Send Conditional Push Notifications To** and choose the activities and roles you want. The mention checkbox does not change these rules. Review existing activity defaults before enabling a wider set of notifications.

To stop all Teams notifications through preferences, turn off the Teams mention checkbox **and** clear the selected rules in the Teams activity grid. Email, Slack and Notification Center preferences remain separate.

See the [complete Teams installation and troubleshooting guide](/workspace-administration/settings/integrations/microsoft-teams) for the app download, administrator steps, missing Add controls, account matching, and registration problems. Test environments also need their own app package and outbound delivery enabled by support.

#### E-mail Notifications 

When a user is assigned a task or when a specific event occurs within the project, the software automatically generates an e-mail notification and sends it to the designated recipient(s). These notifications are typically customizable, allowing users to define their preferences regarding the types of notifications they wish to receive.

E-mail notifications in project management software are designed to ensure that team members stay updated and remain aware of important project developments. They provide timely and relevant information, helping individuals prioritize their work, take necessary actions, and stay aligned with the project's progress.

By leveraging e-mail notifications, team members can easily stay connected with the project, collaborate effectively, and maintain visibility into their responsibilities and upcoming deadlines.

To enable email direct mentions, select **Email** under **Notify Me of Direct Mentions Via** in **My Account → Notification Settings**. Select the separate **Email** tab to configure conditional activity notifications.
