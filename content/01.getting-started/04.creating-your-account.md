---
title: "Creating your account"
description: "Sign up for DevStride with an email address and password, or with your existing Google or Microsoft account."
---

There are two ways to create a DevStride account. Both land you in the same
place — naming your first organization — and you can use either one regardless
of how you were invited.

## Sign up with Google or Microsoft

On the sign-up page, accept the terms and then choose **Sign up with Google** or
**Sign up with Microsoft**. You'll authenticate with that provider and come
straight back to DevStride with your account created — no DevStride password to
choose, and none to forget later.

The buttons stay greyed out until you've agreed to the terms of use and privacy
policy. That's deliberate: choosing a provider sends you straight to them, so
your agreement has to be recorded before you leave.

::callout{icon="i-heroicons-information-circle"}
Only the providers your environment has configured appear. If you don't see a
button for your provider, sign up with an email address and password instead.
::

### Your account needs an email address

DevStride requires a working email address on every account — it's where
notifications go, and it's how invitations find you. Sign-up therefore only
completes if your identity provider tells us your address.

Nearly every Google and personal Microsoft account does. If yours doesn't, the
sign-up is declined with a note pointing you at email-and-password sign-up,
which works exactly as it always has.

### If you already have a DevStride account

Using a provider whose email address already belongs to a DevStride account
doesn't create a second one. You're simply signed in to the account you already
have, and that provider is linked to it for next time — so one person always
has one account, however many ways they sign in.

### Work accounts and single sign-on

If your organization uses single sign-on, you can't create a *new* DevStride
account with your work identity — that route is reserved for people your
administrator has invited. Ask them for an invitation; once you have one,
signing in with SSO works normally.

## Sign up with an email address and password

Fill in your name, email address and a password, accept the terms, and choose
**Create Profile**. You'll confirm your email address, then name your first
organization.

## What happens next

However you signed up, if you don't belong to an organization yet you'll be
asked to create one — you become its owner. If you were invited to an existing
organization before signing up, you'll join it automatically instead.
