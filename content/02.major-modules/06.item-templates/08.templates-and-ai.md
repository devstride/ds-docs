Everything on the Templates page is also available to an AI assistant connected through the [DevStride MCP Server](/devstride-mcp-server/what-is-the-devstride-mcp-server). Your assistant works within the permissions and shares you already have, so it can only see and change the templates you can.

## What your assistant can do

| Capability | Typical ask |
| --- | --- |
| **List and read templates** | *"What templates do we have for launching a product?"* |
| **Create a template** | *"Build a template for our quarterly compliance review, with a date anchor and a team variable."* |
| **Update a template** | *"Add a QA epic to the Compliance-Ready Feature template."* |
| **Capture one from existing work** | *"Turn workstream F12 into a reusable template."* |
| **Share a template** | *"Share the onboarding template with the Delivery team, read-only."* |
| **Preview an apply** | *"What would applying the launch template under I34 create?"* |
| **Apply a template** | *"Apply the onboarding template under Product for Northwind Bank, launching 15 March."* |
| **Check what a config change would break** | *"Which templates use the Customer Tier custom field?"* |

## Two habits worth insisting on

**Ask it to capture, not to compose.** Building a valid template definition from scratch is fiddly — every node needs the right work type for its position, and every reference needs a real ID. Pointing your assistant at existing work that already has the right shape and asking it to capture that, then adjust, is faster and far more reliable. It's the same advice as [Capturing an existing item as a template](/major-modules/item-templates/capturing-a-template), for the same reason.

**Ask it to preview before it applies.** A preview creates nothing and reports every problem an apply would hit — a missing required value, an assignee outside the scoped team, a cadence with no open cycle. It's the cheapest possible check, and it's worth making a standing instruction.

## Things to know

- **Applying returns a job, not items.** For a large template, your assistant kicks off the work and then follows the job to completion. If it reports "applied" and you can't see the items yet, give it a moment and ask it to check the job.
- **A partial apply is possible.** As in the app, one failed node skips its own subtree while its siblings carry on. Ask your assistant to report the failures, not just the successes.
- **Updating a template replaces its whole structure.** There's no partial edit at the API level, so an assistant that doesn't read the current definition first can overwrite work. Asking it to "read the template, then change X" rather than "change X" avoids this.
- **Deleting is permanent** — there's no archive. Items already created from the template are unaffected.
