title: "Shared Lists"
description: "Share a filtered, read-only view of your items with chosen customer companies through the Customer Support Portal, behind sign-in."
---

Shared Lists let you share a curated, read-only view of your items with people outside your organization — clients, contractors, or advisors — through the **Customer Support Portal**. Recipients sign in to the portal to see the list, so you always know who is looking.

A Shared List is built on top of DevStride's saved views. It is a saved view whose visibility is set to **Visible to portal customers**, with an audience of either every portal company or a chosen few.

::alert{type="warning" title="Public links have been retired"}
DevStride used to publish these as **public web links** that anyone could open without signing in. That option is gone. Existing `/stored-view/...` links now show a page explaining the link has been retired — they return no data at all, not even your organization's name.

If you previously shared a list by link, **republish it** using the steps below and give your customers portal access. See [what happened to my public links?](#what-happened-to-my-public-links) below.
::

## Common use cases

- Share a list of active support issues with a client.
- Give a customer a live view of the deliverables you owe them.
- Provide external collaborators an up-to-date view without a DevStride seat.

## Before you start

Sharing a list is an **Items module** feature:

- The share option appears only in the [Manage Items](/major-modules/workstreams-and-work-items/manage-items-table) module. Other modules — Boards, the Map, the Roadmap, My Work, and Analytics — offer a **Views** button for saving internal views, but not portal sharing.
- The **Visible to portal customers** option is shown to organization Admins.
- Your recipients reach the list through the [Customer Support Portal](/major-modules/service-desk/customer-support-portal), so Service Desk must be enabled and the people you're sharing with must be **requesters of a company** with portal access.
- You must apply at least one customization — a filter, column change, or sort — before you can save a view. Until the current view differs from the base view, the save control stays disabled with the tooltip "Apply filter(s) to save this view."

## Share a list

1. Open the **Items** module from the sidebar to open Manage Items.
2. Use the filter and control bar to shape the list you want to share.
3. Save the view.
4. In the **Save view** dialog, enter a **Name** and an optional **Description**. The name is what your customers see.
5. Under visibility, choose **Visible to portal customers**.
6. Under **Portal audience**, choose who sees it:
   - **All companies** — *Visible to signed-in requesters of all portal companies.*
   - **Specific companies** — *Visible to signed-in requesters of the selected companies.* Pick the companies from the list.
7. Click **Save view**.

::alert{type="note" title="Specific companies needs at least one company"}
If you choose **Specific companies** and don't pick any, saving is refused with *"Choose at least one company, or share with all companies"*. This is deliberate — an empty audience would look like a successful share while being visible to nobody.
::

## What your customers see

A signed-in portal customer finds shared lists under **Shared lists** in their portal. They see only lists shared with **their** company — never another customer's.

The list is strictly read-only: they can browse and open items to see a read-only detail view, and they never see the rest of your workspace.

::alert{type="tip" title="Staff-only signals stay staff-only"}
Internal prioritisation signals are stripped from shared rows. Customer Rank and Importance — which record how *other* customers rank their requests — are never sent to a portal viewer.
::

## Changing or revoking access

Because the audience is a property of the saved view, you change who can see a list by editing the view:

- **Narrow it** by switching from All companies to Specific companies, or removing a company.
- **Revoke it entirely** by changing visibility back to **Visible only to you** or **Visible to coworkers**.

Access is re-checked on every portal request, so a change takes effect immediately — you don't wait for anyone's session to expire. Revoking a requester's portal access, blocking them, removing them from their company, or disabling Service Desk all cut off their access to shared lists at the same moment.

## What happened to my public links?

Public view links are retired. Anyone opening an old link sees:

> **This shared link has been retired**
>
> Public view links are no longer available — views are now shared securely with signed-in audiences instead of public links. Ask the person who shared this link with you for a new one.

The page is deliberately inert: it ignores the link's token, makes no network request, and reveals nothing about your organization or the view that used to be there.

**To restore access,** open the view, set its visibility to **Visible to portal customers**, choose the audience, and make sure your recipients are requesters of a company with portal access. If you own views that were publicly published, DevStride can send you a one-time digest listing them so you can republish each one — your administrator arranges when that goes out.
