## Overview

* * *
Trending Progress refers to the overtime patterns of work completed over a given a period of time. The report is designed for project managers to access data that help them manage progress tracking by analyzing the trends within the company through a variety of metrics.

![trending_progress.png](/images/trending_progress.png)

## The time axis

The chart draws nothing into the future: its axis runs from the start of your selected range up to and including the **current period**, which is shown as a partial period holding today's snapshot. Selecting "This Month" on the 2nd therefore shows two days, not thirty columns with two filled. Weekly periods run **Monday to Sunday**. Choosing a range that lies entirely in the future plots nothing.
