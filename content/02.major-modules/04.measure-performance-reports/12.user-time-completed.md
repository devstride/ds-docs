## Overview

* * *
The **User Time Completed** feature, found in the report section, is a valuable tool for tracking productivity and workload. It allows you to select an assignee and view the tasks and/or subtasks they have completed, along with the total hours they've spent on these items, within a specified date range. 

This feature is particularly useful for project managers and team members, as it provides clear insights into individual performance and time management. By using 'User Time Completed', you can ensure that workloads are balanced, identify areas where additional support may be needed, and acknowledge the efforts of team members. It's an essential component for maintaining efficiency and fostering a collaborative and accountable team environment.

The hours shown here come from time logged on individual items. For how that time is recorded and edited, see [time tracking in the item workspace](/getting-started/admin-onboarding-getting-started/item-workspace-tour/right-bar-time-spent).

## Grouping time by job role

In **User Time Tracking**, choose **Assignee role** as the grouping to total time under Engineer, QA Analyst, or another job role. A record belongs to one role, so people with several roles do not multiply the hours. The Excel export includes the recorded job role and role totals once your organization uses roles.

New time records capture the chosen role on the work, falling back to the time author's first active job role when no role is chosen. Changing an assignment or a member's role order later does not move completed time into another role. Older time without a captured role stays **Unspecified**.

Manual subtask time belongs to the subtask's assignee. Editing an existing positive duration keeps its captured role; setting it to zero and then recording time again starts a new capture. Separate time entries retain their own authors and captured roles.

To compare roles month by month over a chosen item hierarchy, use the [Time Allocation Matrix](/major-modules/measure-performance-reports/time-allocation-matrix). Compare the same items, permissions, date range, and timezone: both reports include completed time entries and dated manual subtask time.
