DevStride sends mentions and selected item updates to your **personal chat with the DevStride app in Microsoft Teams**. Setup requires both an organization connection and a personal app installation. Follow the steps below in order, starting with one recipient before rolling out to more people.

## Setup at a glance

| Step | Who does it | How you know it is complete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Connect the Microsoft tenant | A DevStride member with **Manage Integrations**, with access approved by a Microsoft administrator | DevStride shows **Microsoft Teams is connected** and the correct tenant name |
| 2. Make the app available | Your Microsoft Teams administrator | **DevStride** appears under **Apps → Built for your org** in Teams |
| 3. Add the personal app | Each person receiving notifications | **DevStride** is installed and its personal chat opens |
| 4. Choose notifications | Each recipient, in DevStride | **Teams** is selected for direct mentions and/or the desired activity rules |
| 5. Test delivery | A recipient and a second DevStride member | A new mention arrives in Teams and its link opens the correct item |

::alert{type="info" title="Already set up by your administrator?"}
Start at [Install the app for each recipient](#install-the-app-for-each-recipient), then [choose your notifications](#choose-your-notifications). You do not need to connect the organization again or upload another copy of the package.
::

## Before you start

- **DevStride access:** the person connecting the organization needs **Manage Integrations**. Each recipient must have an active membership in that organization and a verified DevStride email address.
- **Microsoft approval:** a Microsoft 365 administrator must approve the connection permission. Once approved, a DevStride member with Manage Integrations can connect using a Microsoft account covered by that approval. That member does not also need to be a Microsoft administrator.
- **Teams access:** recipients must be able to sign in to Teams with a work or school account in the tenant being connected. Their Microsoft subscription/license must include Teams and Teams access must be enabled. Signing in to Azure or Microsoft My Account alone does not confirm this.
- **Matching identity:** the email address Microsoft supplies for the recipient must match their verified DevStride email. A Microsoft sign-in name can differ from the account's email address; ask your administrator to check if they do not match.
- **App access:** your Teams administrator must allow the DevStride app for the intended recipients.

You can use your existing company Microsoft tenant and licensed Teams account. A separate test tenant or purchased domain is not required for ordinary setup. Connecting Teams does not change how you sign in to DevStride; Microsoft SSO is a separate integration.

## Get the app package

**[Download the production DevStride Teams app — version 1.0.2](/downloads/devstride-teams-1.0.2.zip){external download="devstride-teams-1.0.2.zip"}**

Keep the downloaded file as a **ZIP**. Upload the ZIP itself; do not extract it. This is the production package verified with `app.devstride.com`. It contains the app manifest and icons, with no passwords or app secrets.

| DevStride environment | Teams app to install | Package |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Production — `app.devstride.com` | **DevStride** | Use the download above |
| Shared development — `app.devstride.dev` | **DevStride Dev** | Request the current development package from DevStride support |
| Other test environments | The app supplied for that environment | Confirm the package and sending configuration with DevStride support |

Your administrator uploads the package to your organization's catalog; you do not need to find a public Microsoft Teams Store listing. Customers using this integration do not need to create an Azure bot, register an Entra application, or generate a client secret.

## Connect the tenant

**Do this once for each DevStride organization, before installing the personal app.**

1. Sign in to the intended DevStride environment and select the organization you want to connect.
2. Open **Configure Settings → Integrations → Microsoft Teams**.
3. Select **Connect Microsoft Teams**. At Microsoft's account picker, choose the work or school account in the intended company tenant. Check the account carefully if the browser remembers several accounts.
4. Complete Microsoft's sign-in and approval flow in the same browser. The connection requests **Connect Microsoft Teams to DevStride**, basic identity information, and continued access to the approved information. It does not request Microsoft mail or file access.
5. If Microsoft requires approval, have your Microsoft administrator approve access for the connecting account, or for the organization where appropriate. Then complete the connection from DevStride. If approval already exists, Microsoft may return directly without showing another permission prompt.
6. Back in DevStride, confirm **Microsoft Teams is connected** and check **Connected Microsoft Tenant**. The tenant should be the organization in which recipients use Teams.

A successful connection confirms that the connecting account has the required approval. It does not establish that consent was granted to every account in the Microsoft tenant.

::alert{type="info" title="One Microsoft tenant per DevStride organization"}
To switch tenants, remove the existing connection first. If the wrong tenant appears, correct it before installing the app or testing. An organization connection alone does not install the personal app or prove notification delivery.
::

## Add the app in Teams

### Make the app available to your organization

**Your Microsoft Teams administrator completes this step.** The catalog makes the app discoverable; each recipient still needs it installed for personal use.

The following path is available to administrators in Teams web or desktop:

1. Sign in to Teams in the same Microsoft tenant connected in DevStride.
2. Open **Apps → Manage your apps → Upload an app**.
3. Select **Upload an app to your org's app catalog**.
4. Select the production ZIP downloaded above. Wait for the upload to finish.
5. Open **Apps → Built for your org** and find **DevStride**. If you also see **DevStride Dev**, choose **DevStride** for production.
6. Check that your intended recipients are allowed to use this app. Start with the pilot recipients if you are rolling out gradually.

Alternatively, use the [Teams admin center](https://admin.teams.microsoft.com/): open **Teams apps → Manage apps**, then **Upload new app** (or **Actions → Upload new app**, depending on the interface), and upload the ZIP. Microsoft notes that catalog availability can take a few hours. See Microsoft's [custom app administration guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-custom-app-policies-and-settings).

Teams presents different upload choices based on your role and policies:

| Choice shown | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Upload an app to your org's app catalog** | Administrator distribution to your organization; this is the setup path above |
| **Upload a custom app** | Personal or small-pilot installation, if your administrator permits it |
| **Submit an app to your org** | Request administrator review; submitting does not install or approve the app |

If you cannot upload, give the ZIP to your Teams administrator. Users who only need notifications can install the approved catalog app without permission to upload custom apps. Administrators can also use Microsoft's app deployment/setup controls to install it for selected users; verify the personal installation with a pilot recipient before a wider rollout.

### Install the app for each recipient

**Every person who wants Teams notifications completes this step**, including the administrator who uploaded the package.

1. In Teams, use your profile menu to check your work or school account and organization.
2. Open **Apps → Built for your org** and select **DevStride**. Your organization must have completed the catalog step above.
3. Review the app details. The production package is **DevStride**, version **1.0.2**. It uses your basic profile to identify you and can send you messages and notifications. Review the linked terms and privacy policy, then select **Add** for personal use.
4. Open the app when Teams offers **Open**, or select its **DevStride** chat. You can also confirm it appears in **Apps → Manage your apps**.
5. Continue to your DevStride notification preferences below.

::alert{type="success" title="“You cannot send messages to this bot” is expected"}
DevStride is a notification-only app. Its chat can be empty until your first notification arrives, and the message composer is disabled. You do not need to type a command or send a greeting. Notifications arrive in this personal chat, not in a team channel or group chat.
::

Adding the personal app registers your Teams account for delivery. Merely finding it in the catalog or opening an already-installed app does not repeat that registration. If it was added before the tenant connection or during a setup failure, see [troubleshooting](#if-no-message-arrives) before testing again.

## Choose your notifications

**Each recipient chooses their own preferences in the connected DevStride organization.**

1. In DevStride, open your account menu, then **My Account → Notification Settings**.
2. Under **Notify Me of Direct Mentions Via**, check **Teams** to receive messages when another member mentions you. Teams direct mentions are off by default. These preferences save as you change them.
3. Under **Send Conditional Push Notifications To**, select the **Teams** tab. Choose the activities and your role on an item that should trigger a message. For example, check **Assignee Modified** in the **Assignee** column for assignment changes.
4. Review the existing selections; conditional notification defaults can already be enabled. Choose only the events you want.

The direct-mention checkbox and the Teams activity grid are independent. Selecting one does not select the other. You can continue using Email, Slack, and the DevStride Notification Center alongside Teams.

If the Teams options do not appear, check the selected organization and reload the page after its Teams connection is complete. These controls confirm an organization connection, not a personal app installation. See [notification settings](/personal-workspace/notifications/customizing-notification-settings-for-individuals-and-teams) for more examples.

## Verify a notification

Use this check before inviting more users to set up the integration. There is no separate Teams test-message button.

1. Confirm that the recipient has completed the connection, personal installation, email verification, and Teams direct-mention preference above.
2. Have a **second DevStride member** in the same organization and environment open a test item that both people can access. The sender must have permission to add a comment.
3. In a new comment, type **@**, select the recipient from the suggestions, add a short test message, and post it. Typing a name as plain text is not an actual mention. Mentioning yourself does not generate a notification.
4. In the recipient's Teams account, open the personal **DevStride** chat and look for the new mention. A production pilot delivered in seconds, but this is not a delivery-time guarantee.
5. Select **Open mention in DevStride** in the message. For production, it should open the correct item at `app.devstride.com`. A development test should use **DevStride Dev** and open `app.devstride.dev`.

**Setup is complete when the recipient sees the real message and its link opens the correct item.** A catalog entry, an empty bot chat, or the connected label alone is not a delivery test.

To test activity notifications as well, have another member make an assignment or other change covered by the recipient's Teams activity/role selections. To check opt-out behavior, turn off Teams direct mentions, create a new mention from the other account, and verify that it produces no Teams message; restore the preferred setting afterwards.

Always create a **new event** after installation, preference changes, or troubleshooting. Earlier notifications are not a substitute for a fresh test. In a development or sandbox environment, have DevStride support confirm outbound Teams delivery is enabled.

## If no message arrives

| What you see | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Microsoft asks for administrator approval | Have a Microsoft administrator approve the connection for the account being used. Manage Integrations in DevStride and an Azure subscription role do not grant Microsoft consent authority. |
| Microsoft reports that the application/resource is missing from your tenant, including `AADSTS650052` | Contact DevStride support and your Microsoft administrator with the error details. Support may need to help provision the existing DevStride authorization application in your tenant before approval can complete. Do not create a replacement app or secret. |
| Teams says you do not have permission to access the organization | Check the signed-in account and Microsoft tenant, then ask your Microsoft administrator to verify that the account has Teams access and the required license. |
| Upload is missing, or you only see **Submit an app to your org** | Ask your Teams administrator to upload the ZIP to the organization's catalog and allow the app for your account. Submission awaits approval. |
| DevStride is missing from **Built for your org**, or you see **Request** instead of **Add** | Check the tenant, catalog upload, and app availability for your user. Allow catalog/policy changes time to propagate. Your administrator can inspect the app in Teams admin center. |
| Teams shows **Something went wrong** during Add | Check **Manage your apps** first to see whether installation completed. If absent, refresh Teams and retry after catalog/policy changes have propagated. If the admin center reports app-management changes in progress, wait for that process; persistent errors need your Teams administrator or Microsoft support. |
| The chat is empty or says **You cannot send messages to this bot** | This is normal for a newly installed notification-only app. Use the second-member mention test above. |
| The Teams checkbox/tab is missing in DevStride | Check the selected DevStride organization and its connected Microsoft tenant, then reload Notification Settings. |
| Connected and installed, but no message | Confirm the Teams account's email matches the verified DevStride email, the DevStride membership is active, the recipient can access the item, and the sender selected a real @mention. |
| Only some events arrive | Check direct mentions separately from Teams conditional activity and role rules. Use a different member to create a fresh event. |
| The app was added before the organization was connected, or during a configuration failure | Ask DevStride support to check personal registration. After the connection/configuration is repaired, support may ask you to remove and add the personal app again. Reopening it alone does not repeat registration. |
| A development or sandbox environment is silent | Confirm the environment-specific app package and outbound sending configuration with DevStride support. |
| The app is blocked or was removed | Have your Teams administrator allow it, then reinstall it for your account and run a new test. |
| A message opens the wrong environment | Verify that the app package and DevStride environment match. Use **DevStride** for production and the designated test app for development. |

For support, include the DevStride environment and organization, Microsoft tenant name, recipient email, approximate test time and time zone, item number, expected notification, and any Microsoft error/correlation ID. Send these through your support channel; do not send passwords or app secrets.

## Disconnect

To stop Teams delivery for the selected DevStride organization, open **Configure Settings → Integrations → Microsoft Teams**, select **Remove**, and confirm **Remove Connection**. Other DevStride organizations have their own connections.

To stop your own Teams notifications, turn off Teams direct mentions and clear the applicable Teams activity rules in **My Account → Notification Settings**. Removing the personal app in Teams also stops delivery to that installation; it does not remove other users' personal installations.
