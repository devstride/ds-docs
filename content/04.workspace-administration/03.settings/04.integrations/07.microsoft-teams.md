## What the integration does

DevStride can send notifications to a **personal chat with the DevStride app in Microsoft Teams**. These are personal messages, not posts to a team channel or group chat. The app is notification-only; you do not need to send it a message or enter a command.

Setup has three parts: connect your organization's Microsoft tenant, add the app in Teams for each recipient, and choose which notifications they receive. A **Microsoft Teams is connected** label confirms the tenant connection; it does not prove that a message has been delivered.

::alert{type="info" title="Availability and app packages"}
Contact DevStride support to confirm Teams is enabled for your environment and obtain its current app package. A test environment uses its own app, such as **DevStride Dev**, and its own connection. Use the production package only with the production environment.
::

## Before you start

- The person connecting the organization needs **Manage Integrations** in DevStride.
- A Microsoft 365 administrator must approve the requested access before the first connection. After approval, an authorized DevStride member can connect; the connecting member does not have to be a Microsoft administrator if the approval covers their account.
- Recipients need access to Teams in the **connected Microsoft tenant** and permission to install the app. Azure portal access alone does not establish Teams access.
- The email address Microsoft supplies for each recipient must match their **verified DevStride email**, and they must be an active member of the connected DevStride organization.

::alert{type="info" title="One tenant per organization"}
An organization connects to one Microsoft tenant. To switch tenants, disconnect the current one first. Check the organization selected in Teams, especially if you belong to several Microsoft tenants.
::

## Connect the tenant

1. In DevStride, open **Configure Settings → Integrations → Microsoft Teams**.
2. Select **Connect Microsoft Teams** and sign in with the intended Microsoft work or school account. Complete the connection in the same browser.
3. Review Microsoft's permission prompt. The connection requests permission to **Connect Microsoft Teams to DevStride**, basic profile information, and continued access to the approved information. This flow does not request access to your mail or files.
4. If Microsoft requires administrator approval, ask your Microsoft administrator to approve access. An administrator can approve for their own account or, where appropriate, on behalf of the organization. A successful connection alone does not mean organization-wide consent was granted.
5. Back in DevStride, verify **Microsoft Teams is connected** and the displayed tenant name.

## Add the app in Teams

Your Teams administrator can distribute the package through the organization's app catalog or an app setup policy. Each recipient needs the app added for **personal use**.

For a small pilot, if your tenant permits custom app uploads:

1. In Teams desktop or web, open **Apps → Manage your apps → Upload an app → Upload a custom app**.
2. Select the ZIP package supplied by DevStride for this environment.
3. Choose **Add**, then **Open** for personal use.

If upload or installation is unavailable, ask your Teams administrator to check the policies assigned to you. Microsoft's [custom app upload instructions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/platform/concepts/deploy-and-publish/apps-upload) describe the current controls and prerequisites.

Adding the app registers your account for delivery. Simply opening an already-installed app or typing into its chat does not repeat registration. Install it after connecting the tenant. If support repairs a failed registration, they may ask you to remove and add the app again.

## Choose your notifications

In the same DevStride organization, open your account menu and select **My Account → Notification Settings**:

- Under **Notify Me of Direct Mentions Via**, select **Teams** if you want messages when another member mentions you. Direct mentions are off by default for Teams.
- Under **Send Conditional Push Notifications To**, select the **Teams** tab and choose the item activities and recipient roles you want. For example, enable **Assignee Modified** for the **Assignee** role to receive assignment changes.

The Teams tab appears when your organization has a connected tenant. It does not check whether you have installed the app. Defaults can already enable conditional notifications, so review your preferences before testing. See [notification settings](/personal-workspace/notifications/customizing-notification-settings-for-individuals-and-teams) for the other channels and options.

## Verify a notification

There is no dedicated Teams test-message button. Use a small test item and a second DevStride member so the test exercises the normal notification flow:

1. Confirm the tenant connection, personal app installation, matching email address, and Teams preference above. For a test environment, have DevStride support confirm outbound Teams delivery is enabled.
2. Enable **Teams** for direct mentions. Ask another member of the **same DevStride organization and environment** to mention you in a comment on a test item you can access. A self-mention is excluded from notifications.
3. Look for the notification in your personal chat with **DevStride** (or the environment's test app, such as **DevStride Dev**).
4. Open the item link from the message. It should open the correct item in the correct DevStride environment; a shared-dev test should lead to `app.devstride.dev`.
5. To verify your preference is respected, turn off Teams direct mentions and have the other member create a new mention. That new mention should not produce a Teams message. Restore your preferred setting afterwards.

You can also test an assignment change made by another member, provided the Teams activity and recipient-role selections cover that change. Generate a **new** event after setup or troubleshooting; do not rely on an earlier notification being replayed.

## If no message arrives

| What you see | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Microsoft asks for approval | A Microsoft administrator must approve access for the connecting account. DevStride permissions and Azure subscription ownership are separate from Microsoft consent authority. |
| Teams option is unavailable | Check the DevStride organization and its connected tenant. Reload your preferences after connecting. |
| Connected, but no message | Confirm the app is added for personal use in that Microsoft tenant, and the Microsoft email matches a verified, active DevStride member. |
| Only some events arrive | Review the Teams direct-mention setting separately from the Teams conditional activity and role selections. Use another member to create a new test event. |
| Test environment is silent | Ask support to confirm outbound Teams sending is enabled for that environment. A tenant connection alone does not enable test-environment delivery. |
| App was added before a configuration problem was repaired | Ask support to check registration. Removing and adding the app again can register it after the repair; reopening it alone does not. |
| App is blocked or removed | Ask your Teams administrator to allow the app, then follow support's reconnect or reinstall guidance. |
| Message arrives but its link is wrong | Verify that the app package and DevStride environment match. A localhost link works only on the computer hosting that frontend. |

When contacting support, include the environment, organization, approximate test time, item number, and which notification you expected. Do not send passwords or app secrets.

## Disconnect

Under **Configure Settings → Integrations → Microsoft Teams**, select **Remove** next to the connected tenant. This stops Teams delivery for that DevStride organization. Removing the app from your own Teams account stops your personal delivery; other recipients have their own installations.
