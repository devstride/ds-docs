## Overview

The Microsoft Teams integration connects your DevStride organization to one Microsoft 365 tenant. Connecting registers the tenant with DevStride and installs the DevStride bot for it; that is the foundation for delivering notifications into Teams, which arrives in a later release.

::alert{type="info" title="One tenant per organization"}
An organization connects to a single Microsoft tenant. To switch tenants, disconnect the current one first.
::

## Connecting a tenant

You need the **Manage Integrations** permission in DevStride and the ability to grant **admin consent** in your Microsoft tenant.

1. Open **Settings** from the bottom of the left-hand sidebar.
2. Select **Integrations**, then choose **Microsoft Teams** from the list on the left.
3. Click **Connect Microsoft Teams**. You are sent to Microsoft to sign in and grant consent for the DevStride app; complete it in the same browser you started from.
4. Back in DevStride, the page shows the **connected tenant** by name.

The DevStride app must also be installed in your Teams environment for the bot to reach people; the settings page links to the distribution guidance for your Teams administrator.

## Disconnecting

On the same page, click **Remove** next to the connected tenant. DevStride forgets the tenant and its credentials; you can connect again at any time.
