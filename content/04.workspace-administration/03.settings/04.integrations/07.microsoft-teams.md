## Overview

The Microsoft Teams integration connects your DevStride organization to one Microsoft 365 tenant. Connecting registers the tenant with DevStride and installs the DevStride bot for it, which is what lets DevStride deliver notifications into Teams.

## What members receive

Once the tenant is connected, each member who **adds the DevStride bot in Teams** (a personal chat with the bot) receives their DevStride notifications as Teams messages: assignments, status changes, comments, direct mentions and due-date reminders, according to the **Teams** tab of their [notification preferences](/personal-workspace/notifications/overview-of-notification-options). The Teams tab appears only for organizations with a connected tenant. Adding the bot is what registers a member for delivery — sending the bot a message does not — so a member who never added it receives nothing in Teams and keeps their other channels unchanged. Teams defaults match Slack's: work-item notifications on, direct mentions off.

If Microsoft or a tenant administrator later blocks the app, DevStride stops sending to that tenant until an administrator reconnects it; a member who removes the bot simply stops receiving Teams messages until they add it again.

::alert{type="info" title="One tenant per organization"}
An organization connects to a single Microsoft tenant. To switch tenants, disconnect the current one first.
::

## Connecting a tenant

You need the **Manage Integrations** permission in DevStride and the ability to grant **admin consent** in your Microsoft tenant.

1. Open **Settings** from the bottom of the left-hand sidebar.
2. Select **Integrations**, then choose **Microsoft Teams** from the list on the left.
3. Click **Connect Microsoft Teams**. You are sent to Microsoft to sign in and grant consent for the DevStride app; complete it in the same browser you started from.
4. Back in DevStride, the page shows the **connected tenant** by name.

The DevStride app must also be installed in your Teams environment for the bot to reach people; the settings page links to the distribution guidance for your Teams administrator.

## Disconnecting

On the same page, click **Remove** next to the connected tenant. DevStride forgets the tenant and its credentials; you can connect again at any time.
