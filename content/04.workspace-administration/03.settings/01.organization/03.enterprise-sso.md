Enterprise SSO connects DevStride to **your organization's own identity provider** (IdP) — Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace, or anything that speaks OIDC or SAML. Once configured, anyone who types a company email address on the DevStride login page is offered a **"Continue with [your company] SSO"** button that sends them through your own corporate login and straight back into your organization.

Requires the **Owner** role (or a role granted the manage-identity-providers permission).

## Connecting an identity provider

1. In the left sidebar, click **Settings**, then **Organization**, then **Identity Providers**.
2. Click **New Identity Provider** and choose a protocol:
   - **OIDC** (recommended) — needs the **Issuer URL**, **Client ID**, and **Client Secret** from an app registration in your IdP. The issuer must be your organization's own **single-tenant** issuer — for Microsoft Entra, `https://login.microsoftonline.com/<your-tenant-guid>/v2.0`. Multi-tenant issuers (`/common`, `/organizations`, `/consumers`) accept sign-ins from outside your organization and are rejected.
   - **SAML** — needs your IdP's **metadata** (URL or XML). The protocol choice is permanent for a provider; sign-in security is the same either way.
3. In your IdP, register DevStride with the redirect URI `https://login.devstride.com/oauth2/idpresponse`.
4. Add the **email domains** your people sign in with (for example `acme.com`), then verify each one — see below.

## Verifying your email domain

The domain claim is what routes sign-ins to your provider, so DevStride requires **proof that your organization controls the domain**, verified by DNS:

1. Next to the claimed domain, DevStride shows a **DNS challenge record** — a TXT record at `_devstride-challenge.<your-domain>` with a unique value.
2. Create that record with your DNS provider.
3. Click **Verify**. Once the record is found, the domain is marked verified and sign-in routing turns on.

A domain can only be verified by one identity provider across all of DevStride — verification is what claims it. Unverified claims don't route anything and don't block anyone else.

## What your team sees

On the DevStride login page, typing an email address on a verified domain replaces the password field with your organization's SSO button. Everything else — unclaimed domains, other organizations — sees the ordinary login page, unchanged. Members with an existing DevStride password keep it; their first SSO sign-in simply links the corporate identity to the same account.

## Automatic member provisioning (JIT)

With **automatic provisioning** enabled on a provider that holds a verified domain, someone from your company who has never been invited can sign in through SSO and get a DevStride account created on the spot, joined to your organization with the **default role you choose**. Provisioning is limited to email addresses on your verified domains — it can never create accounts for another organization's domain.

::alert{type="note" title="Which Microsoft option do I want?"}
The **Sign in with Microsoft** button on the login page serves **personal** Microsoft accounts (outlook.com and friends). Company **work accounts** in your Entra tenant are served by Enterprise SSO on this page — the two don't overlap.
::
