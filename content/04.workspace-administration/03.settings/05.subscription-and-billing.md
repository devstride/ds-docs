The **Subscription** area inside Organization Settings is where you manage everything related to your organization's plan and payments. Billing in DevStride is powered by [Stripe](https://stripe.com), so your saved cards, billing address, and invoices are all handled securely through Stripe.

The Subscription area is divided into four sub-pages, in this order:

- **Subscription Tier** — view available plans, switch tiers, or cancel
- **Billing Details** — manage the billing contact and address used for invoices
- **Payment Method** — add, remove, and set the active credit card
- **Billing History** — review and download past invoices

::alert{type="info" title="Restricted to roles with settings access"}
The entire Organization Settings area — including Subscription & Billing — appears only for roles whose permissions include access to organization settings (billing additionally requires the **Manage billing** permission). By default that's the **Owner** and **Admin** roles; standard Members are redirected to My Account and never see these pages. Roles are configurable in [Configure Settings → Permissions](/permissions/roles-and-permissions). Subscriptions are managed per organization.
::

## New workspaces: a free DevStride trial

A workspace created from 24 September 2026 onwards starts on a **free 14-day trial run by DevStride**, with no Stripe subscription behind it. Nothing is charged during the trial.

When you open **Subscription**, the first page is **Your Plan** instead of **Subscription Tier**. It shows **Free trial until** a date, and the price reads **Pricing to be confirmed** until DevStride sets your plan.

### Setting up card payments

People who can manage billing can enrol from **Your Plan**:

1. Save a card on the page.
2. Enter the billing contact: the person and email that should receive billing messages.
3. Read and accept the authorisation, which lets DevStride charge the card each month on your bill day.
4. Press **Start card payments**.

Your Plan then shows how you pay, for example **By card, charged monthly on the bill day**.

A week before the trial ends, the workspace's Owners get one email, and the people who manage billing see a prompt to set up payment.

::alert{type="info" title="Annual terms, invoices or ACH"}
Card enrolment is monthly. If you'd rather pay annually, by invoice or by ACH, [contact DevStride](mailto:sales@devstride.com) and we'll set it up for you.
::

The rest of this page describes workspaces billed through Stripe, which covers every workspace created before 24 September 2026.

## Opening the Subscription area

1. Click **Settings** at the bottom of the left sidebar to expand the settings menu.
2. Expand the **Subscription** group in the settings navigation.
3. Choose one of the four sub-pages: **Subscription Tier**, **Billing Details**, **Payment Method**, or **Billing History**.

The page opens on **Subscription Tier** by default, and your selection is reflected in the page's address (the `?section=` portion of the URL).

## Subscription Tier

The **Subscription Tier** page shows a pricing card for each available plan, plus an **Enterprise** card. Each plan card shows:

- The price, formatted per user per month or per user per year
- The plan's limits — **Assets Storage** (shown per user), **Automation Actions**, and **AI Smart Suggestions**

::alert{type="note" title="Plans come from Stripe"}
The plans, prices, and limits shown on this page are pulled dynamically from your organization's Stripe products, so the exact tiers and values you see depend on your account.
::

Use the **Billed Annually** / **Billed Monthly** toggle to switch between billing frequencies. Annual billing shows a savings stamp.

The action button on each card reflects its relationship to your current plan:

- **Current Plan** — the plan you are on now
- **Upgrade** — move to a higher tier
- **Downgrade** — move to a lower tier
- **Select** — choose this plan
- **Contact Us** — shown on the **Enterprise** card; opens a contact request so the DevStride team can follow up (Enterprise and custom plans are not self-serve)

### Changing your tier

While your subscription is active, you can switch tiers directly from the plan cards. Selecting a different tier opens a **Change Subscription** confirmation dialog (confirm with **Accept Change**). How the change takes effect depends on the direction:

- **Upgrades** are charged a prorated amount immediately.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
- **During a trial**, the new tier applies for the rest of the trial; your card is charged when the trial ends.

If you schedule a change (such as a downgrade), a banner appears showing the pending change with a **Cancel Change** button so you can reverse it before it takes effect.

### When your subscription is not active

A workspace billed through Stripe can no longer start a new subscription on its own. When your subscription is not active, the page shows your trial days remaining or a status message, and one of these:

- **A trial with days left and no card saved** — an **Add a payment method** button, which opens the **Payment Method** page. Save a card before the trial ends to keep using DevStride.
- **A trial with a card saved** — nothing to do: you're billed automatically when the trial ends.
- **A trial that has ended, or a subscription that was canceled or suspended** — a **Contact us to reactivate** button. Email [support@devstride.com](mailto:support@devstride.com) with your workspace name and we'll set it up so you can keep using DevStride.

### Canceling and reinstating

Click **Cancel Subscription** to start a cancellation. A confirmation dialog appears (confirm with **Cancel Subscription**, or back out with **Nevermind**).

::alert{type="info" title="Access continues until the period ends"}
Canceling keeps your access active until the end of the current billing period. After you cancel, a banner shows the scheduled cancellation date along with a **Reinstate Subscription** button if you change your mind.
::

### Subscription reminders

When something needs attention on your subscription, DevStride shows a reminder as soon as you open it:

- **During a DevStride trial with no way to pay set up**, people who manage billing see a prompt with **Set up payment** and **Continue trial**.
- **When a payment fails**, the reminder asks you to update your payment method. While your access continues it appears at most once a day and can be closed; once access is suspended it appears every time until the payment method is updated.
- **When your subscription has been cancelled**, the reminder says so and asks you to email [support@devstride.com](mailto:support@devstride.com) to reactivate your workspace.

Members who can't manage billing see the failed-payment and cancellation reminders without the payment buttons, and while access is suspended they're asked to contact an administrator. The reminder always offers **Sign Out**, and **Change Organization** if you belong to more than one.

### Custom plans

If your organization is on a custom (non-standard) plan, the page displays a message that you are on a custom plan and should contact support to make changes, with a link to [sales@devstride.com](mailto:sales@devstride.com). Enterprise and custom plans are not self-serve.

## Billing Details

The **Billing Details** page hosts a Stripe-powered form for the billing contact used on your invoices. It collects:

- An **Email** address
- First and last name
- A phone number
- A billing address (address line 1 and 2, city, state, postal code, and country)

Save your changes with the **Update Billing Details** button. A confirmation appears once the billing contact is updated. The form is rendered by Stripe and adapts to your light or dark theme.

## Payment Method

The **Payment Method** page, headed **Credit Cards**, lists the cards saved to your account. Each card shows its brand icon, an optional cardholder name, the last four digits, and its expiration date (**Expires on MM/YY**). A card that has passed its expiration date is flagged with a red **Expired** tag.

- The card currently used for charges shows an **Active Card** badge.
- Any other valid card shows a **Make Active** button so you can switch which card is charged.

To add a card, open the **Add Card** row to expand a Stripe payment form. Enter the cardholder name and email, complete the card details, and submit with **Add Card**.

To remove a card, use the **x** icon and confirm in the **Delete Card** dialog.

::alert{type="warning" title="You can't delete your only card"}
On an active subscription you cannot delete your last remaining card. If you try, DevStride asks you to add a new card first. You can delete a sole card only once the subscription has been canceled.
::

## Billing History

The **Billing History** page is a read-only table of your Stripe invoices, with these columns:

- **Invoice number**
- **Amount** — formatted with the correct currency; a tooltip shows **Discount** or **Applied amount** where applicable
- **Created Date**
- **Status**
- A download action for the invoice PDF

The **Status** badge maps each invoice to one of the following:

- **Paid** (green)
- **Failed Payment** (red)
- **Partially Refunded**
- **Fully Refunded**
- **Incomplete Payment**

Rows load as you scroll (20 at a time). Click the download icon to open the invoice PDF in a new browser tab.
